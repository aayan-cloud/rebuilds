Rebuilds

Ten projects that were either abandoned or briefly famous, rebuilt as single HTML files. No build step, no dependencies to install, no accounts. Each one runs from a browser and holds 60fps on a mid-range phone. Add ?peak=1 to any of them to see it under deliberate overload.
